# Join Base44 with Mandy

Base44 is a platform where you build apps and personal AI agents by describing them in plain language. No coding needed. You type what you want, and the agent builds it, connects services, and runs tasks for you.

## Your invite link

Start here:

https://app.base44.com/register?ref=I8RJJA1M1STTJFGS&from_url=%2Fcreate-superagent

The link opens a normal sign-up page. Choose how you want to sign in, and you land straight in the builder.

## What you get

Free credits to start, added to the new account when you register through the invite link. That is enough to build a real app, not just a toy.

If you later upgrade to a paid plan, the person who invited you earns bonus credits. Bonus credits are subject to the referral terms shown on the "Earn bonus credits" screen inside the app.

## What you can build

An app with pages, a database, users and payments, all from a chat window.
A personal AI agent with its own workspace, tools and memory, that can answer on WhatsApp, send email, run scheduled jobs and build Android apps.
Automations that run on a schedule or when something changes in your data.

## How to start

1. Open the invite link above and register.
2. Describe what you want to build, in your own words.
3. Let the agent build it, then ask for changes the same way.

That is the whole loop. Nothing to install on your computer.

## Good to know

Credits are included on every plan, and plan limits differ. Check the current plans and credits on the Base44 site.
Security, privacy and data handling are documented by Base44 at base44.com/security.
